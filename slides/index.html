Part 1 · The traps

Detection accuracy is a solved-looking problem

Task (public IDS benchmarks)Typical accuracy
Binary detection (benign vs attack)0.99+
Multi-class attack classification0.77 – 0.97
The question in operations is no longer "can the model detect?" — it is "can the analyst act on this output?"
  • Every alert triggers work: triage, investigation, potential blocking
  • Acting on a wrong-but-confident output costs more than a miss
숫자 자체는 기존 벤치마크 일반론. "정확도는 이미 높다, 문제는 신뢰다"로 프레임 전환.
Part 1 · Trap 1/3

Overconfidence — wrong ≠ uncertain

  • Softmax probability is not P(correct) — it is a trained artifact
  • Cleanly separable training data ⇒ cross-entropy drives outputs to 0.99+
  • Calibration breaks silently: no error message, no exception
A model can be 99% "confident" on inputs unlike anything it was trained on. Confidence measures distance from a learned boundary, not truth.
"모델은 자기가 모른다는 걸 모른다." ECE 언급은 다음 개념 파트에서.
Part 1 · Trap 2/3

Shortcut learning — the fingerprint trap

  • Models latch onto the easiest separating feature, not the true cause
  • In traffic data: TLS fingerprints, port patterns, capture-time artifacts
  • The shortcut holds in the dataset — and inverts in the wild (evasion, benign look-alikes)
Our lab dataset has a shortcut planted by design — so today you will see exactly what happens when reality breaks it. (And that is why we can explain every number you'll see.)
합성 데이터임을 당당하게 공개 — 메커니즘을 설계했으므로 "왜"까지 설명 가능하다는 게 오히려 강점.
Part 1 · Trap 3/3

Distribution shift — and the label problem

  • Traffic drifts: new apps, new libraries, new attack tooling
  • Accuracy in production is invisible — nobody labels live traffic
  • So the one metric you optimized is the one metric you cannot watch
Accuracy ≠ Reliability. Operations need signals that are computable without labels — and that fail loudly when the model fails.
Part 1 요약. "라벨 없이 계산 가능하고, 모델이 망가질 때 시끄럽게 망가지는 신호" = 오늘의 주제.

Part 2 · Concept 1/4

Confidence & calibration

  • Calibrated: among outputs with confidence 0.9, ~90% are correct
  • Measured by ECE / reliability diagrams — on labeled data
  • Deep nets are systematically miscalibrated toward overconfidence
Even a perfectly calibrated model only tells you the average risk — it cannot flag which prediction is the wrong one.
수식 없이 개념만. reliability diagram은 말로 설명 ("x축 confidence, y축 실제 정답률, 대각선이 이상적").
Part 2 · Concept 1/4

What miscalibration looks like

  • The red gap is invisible at run time
  • It is widest exactly where you trust most: the high-confidence zone
  • Demo 1 puts real numbers on this curve
그림 하나로 ECE 직관 전달. "빨간 간극은 런타임에 안 보인다."

Part 2 · Concept 2/4

Uncertainty — what kind of "don't know"?

Aleatoric
Noise in the world. Two flows, identical features, different labels. Irreducible.
Epistemic
Model ignorance. Inputs unlike training data. Reducible — if you can detect it.
  • Drift and evasion live in the epistemic box
  • A single model's softmax cannot see its own ignorance
"모델 하나는 자신의 무지를 못 본다" → 다음 슬라이드에서 앙상블로 연결.
Part 2 · Concept 2/4

A practical proxy: ensemble agreement

  • Train K experts on different evidence (feature subsets, modalities)
  • Agreement = fraction of experts backing the majority decision
  • Familiar inputs → experts converge · unfamiliar inputs → experts scatter
Case in point: verification-aware IDS designs (e.g. VALIDS) emit agreement, evidence-flip and diversity telemetry as first-class outputs — signals you can compute without a single label.
Our lab: 40 small experts, only 10 of which may see the shortcut feature.
VALIDS는 사례 1장으로만. 우리 랩 구성(40 expert, shortcut 노출 10개 제한 = 다양성 제약) 설명.

Part 2 · Concept 3/4

Selective prediction & abstention

  • Stop treating every prediction equally — let the model decline
  • Coverage ↔ risk trade-off: answer less, be right more
  • The interesting question: which score decides acceptance?
An abstained prediction is not a failure. It is a correctly routed decision — to a human, to sandboxing, to more data.
abstention 경로가 있어야 selective prediction이 의미 있음 → Part 4 운영 트리 예고.
Part 2 · buffer

Why precision 1.0 matters in a SOC

  • Auto-blocking on false alarms = self-inflicted outage
  • So automation is gated on the alerts you can trust completely
  • Every extra TP inside the trusted zone = one incident handled at machine speed
The size of that trusted zone is an operational KPI — and it depends entirely on the quality of your acceptance signal.

Part 2 · Concept 4/4

Explainability — necessary, not sufficient

  • Post-hoc XAI (saliency, SHAP, …) describes a decision after the fact
  • An explanation can be plausible and still unverifiable
  • Operations need explanations you can test, not admire
"The model looked at these bytes" is a story.
"Remove this evidence and the decision flips" is a testable claim.
설명 가능 ≠ 검증 가능. 다음 장에서 최소 증거 집합으로 구체화.
Part 2 · Concept 4/4

Evidence-based verification

  • Minimal evidence set: smallest feature subset that carries the decision
  • Sufficiency — evidence alone reproduces the decision
  • Necessity — removing it flips the decision
  • Diverse evidence across experts ⇒ no single shortcut owns the verdict
This is the design idea behind verification-aware IDS models: make the model emit its evidence — then audit the evidence, not the vibes.
VALIDS 충분성/필요성 개념을 일반화해 소개. 깊이는 여기까지 (아키텍처 상세 금지).
buffer · drift in the wild

Where drift actually comes from

  • App & TLS library updates change fingerprints overnight
  • Infrastructure moves: NAT, CDN, proxy rollouts
  • Seasonal and workload cycles
  • Attackers updating tooling — the adversarial kind
CIC-IDS2017 packs this into a single week — by Friday, the model faces attack families it has never seen.

Accept / Review / Withhold

Condition (example policy)Route
Agreement ≥ high bar (our lab: 0.775 ⇒ precision 1.0) Accept — automate
Agreement mid-range, evidence stable Review — analyst triage
Agreement low, or signals disagree with confidence Withhold — abstain, collect, escalate

Part 4 · Operations

Deployment checklist

  • ☐ Measure calibration on held-out data — assume it is broken until shown otherwise
  • ☐ Design the abstention path before turning on automation
  • ☐ Emit label-free telemetry (agreement, evidence stability) from day one
  • ☐ Watch signal trends, not single values
  • ☐ Pin everything — env, data, model, expected numbers — like we did today
체크리스트 낭독은 빠르게. 마지막 항목이 아티팩트/재현성 철학 연결.
Part 4 · Honest limits

What today's lab does not prove

  • Lab 1 is synthetic by design — it demonstrates failure mechanisms, not real-world rates
  • Agreement is not a silver bullet — you watched Friday's novel attacks fool all 40 experts at once, live, on real data
  • Lab 2 is one dataset, one week — thresholds and rates do not transfer; recalibrate on your traffic
  • Generic ensembles ≠ verification-aware models — evidence-level telemetry is the next step up
  • Agent-driven runs add their own variance — verify outputs, always
한계를 먼저 말하면 질문 절반이 사라진다. 4번째 불릿이 VALIDS류 연구 필요성으로 연결되는 다리.
